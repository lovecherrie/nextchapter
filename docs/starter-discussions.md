# Starter discussion drafts

These are original prompts for the owner to review. They are not copied reader reviews or claims that an invented reader finished a book. None have been published.

- **Ward D:** Did the ending work for you, or did it leave you with more questions? Mark the discussion as containing spoilers.
- **The Housemaid:** Would you recommend this as someone's first thriller? What should they read after it?
- **Never Lie:** What kept you reading: the mystery, the characters, or something else? Keep plot details in spoiler-marked replies.
- **Animal Farm:** Which idea from the book feels most relevant today?
- **One Piece, Volume 1:** Would you recommend starting with the manga or the anime? What makes your choice work better for a new reader?
- **The Odyssey:** Which translation would you recommend to someone reading it for the first time?

Publish approved prompts transparently from the owner's real account or an explicitly labelled aepilog account. Do not invent users, votes, replies or claims of personal reading experience.

For material from elsewhere, use content the author has permitted, content under a compatible licence, or short excerpts whose reuse is permitted in context. Keep the author, platform and direct source link visible, honour applicable licence terms, and label it as an excerpt from elsewhere. Attribution by itself does not grant permission to copy a whole review or comment thread. A link accompanied by an original question is another option when the text cannot be reused.
